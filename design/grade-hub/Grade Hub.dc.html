1Grade Hub, Plan tab redesign
Design notes and assumptions
The hero card answers the one question once; the prediction sidebar, per-component needs list, header subtitle, and bottom buffer bar are all absorbed into it. The target grade is an inline chip inside the hero sentence itself, since the target is part of the question. "Sync to study plan" lives in the hero card as the secondary action. The What-if toggle is cut from Plan entirely; that is Sandbox's job. Save sits in the components card header, pinned to the input it saves, not a full-width bar. Math basis: A+ cutoff 90.0, Midterm 93 at 30% earns 27.9 points, leaving 88.7% needed on the remaining 70%, so the cushion is 11.3 points to a perfect 100. Weight total turns amber and the save button disables when weights do not sum to 100 (spec'd in 1a's table header state note). Front-Loaded carries a quiet recommended mark, tweakable via props.
