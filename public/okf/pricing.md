# StudyEdge AI Pricing

## Free Plan

- **Price:** $0/month — no credit card required
- **Courses:** 1 course
- **AI actions:** 5 per month
- **Session Blueprints:** 1 free use
- **Focus Mode:** up to 30 minutes per day
- **AI Coach Plan:** 1 free use
- **Flashcards:** 1 deck, up to 10 cards
- **Grade tracking:** Included

## Pro Plan

- **Monthly:** $9.99/month
- **Annual:** $69.99/year (~$5.83/month, save 42%)
- **Trial:** 7-day free trial, cancel anytime
- **Courses:** Up to 5 courses
- **AI Tutor:** 100 AI study boosts/month
- **Session Blueprints:** Unlimited
- **Focus Mode:** Unlimited
- **AI Study Coach:** Included
- **Smart calendar:** Google Calendar sync
- **Grade Hub:** Full grade tracking and prediction

## Unlimited Plan

- **Monthly:** $14.99/month
- **Annual:** $119.99/year (~$10/month, save 33%)
- **Courses:** Unlimited courses
- **AI Tutor:** Unlimited with session memory
- **Session Blueprints:** Unlimited
- **Focus Mode:** Unlimited
- **Practice Exam Analytics:** Advanced analytics
- **Predicted Exam Score:** AI-powered score prediction
- **Everything in Pro:** Included
