The short answer

If you are in high school preparing for AP exams or the SAT, or you want the most you can get for free, Knowt is hard to beat, and its one-click Quizlet import is something we do not offer. If you are in college with several courses, midterms and finals stacking up, StudyEdge AI is the better fit: it plans each week around your exam dates, runs your study sessions, and tracks your grades.

Choose Knowt if

You want free and exam prep

  • You are studying for AP, SAT, ACT or IB exams
  • You want unlimited files and collaboration for free
  • You want to bring your Quizlet sets with you
  • You need a native iPhone or Android app today

Choose StudyEdge AI if

You are juggling a college semester

  • You have several courses with exams on different dates
  • You want each day's study planned for you
  • You want to track grades and what you need on the final
  • You want cards made from your own lectures and slides

When Knowt is the better pick

Pick Knowt if price is the deciding factor. Its free plan includes unlimited file creation, live collaboration and storage, which is more than our free plan gives you. If you have years of Quizlet sets, its Chrome extension brings them over in one click.

Knowt is also the stronger choice for standardized tests: it has AP, SAT and ACT prep, FRQs, mock exams and an AP score calculator. And it has native iOS and Android apps today.

When StudyEdge AI is the better pick

Pick StudyEdge AI if you are in college and the problem is the semester as a whole. It builds a weekly schedule across all your courses from your exam dates and free time, and each session comes with a plan for what to cover. Flashcards and practice exams are made from your own lectures and slides, so they match what your professor covered.

Grade Hub tracks where you stand in every course and what you need on the work that is left, which Knowt's site does not describe.

Who each one is really built for

Knowt's strongest material is for exams that are the same for everyone: AP, SAT, ACT and IB. Its library and practice tests work because thousands of students are preparing for the same test.

College is different. Your Organic Chemistry midterm is written by your professor, on your syllabus, on a date that collides with three other deadlines. StudyEdge AI is built for that: it works from your own course material and your own calendar, and spends its effort on deciding what you should study this week rather than on a shared library.

Can you use both?

Yes. A student could keep Knowt for free flashcards and shared sets, and use StudyEdge AI to plan the semester and track grades. There is no import between them, so StudyEdge AI makes its own cards from your notes.

Questions

Is Knowt free?

Knowt has a free Basic plan with unlimited file creation, live collaboration and storage, according to knowt.com/plans. Its Ultra plan was $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year when we checked on October 30, 2026, with a trial of three days on the annual plan.

Is StudyEdge AI free?

StudyEdge AI has a free plan with 1 course, 5 AI actions a month and one try of each premium tool, no card needed. Pro is $9.99 a month or $69.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial.

Can I import my Quizlet sets?

Knowt can, with a one-click import through its Chrome extension. StudyEdge AI cannot; it generates flashcards from your own notes, slides and recordings instead.

Which is better for AP exams?

Knowt. It has dedicated AP, SAT and ACT prep, FRQs and mock exams. StudyEdge AI is built for college courses and does not offer standardized-test prep material.
