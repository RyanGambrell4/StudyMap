How AP exams are scored

Most AP exams combine a multiple-choice section and a free-response section into a composite score, which is converted to the 1 to 5 scale. The College Board does not publish fixed cutoffs in advance, so any calculator's conversion, including this one, is an estimate.

Multiple choice section

AP multiple choice questions are scored with no penalty for wrong answers. Your raw MC score is simply the number of questions you answered correctly. Each question is worth one raw point.

Free response section

Free response sections vary significantly by exam. AP Calculus AB has six free-response questions, AP English Language has three essays, and APUSH includes a document-based question and a long essay question, each scored with its own rubric. This calculator uses simplified estimates based on published scoring guidelines.

The composite score

Each course's Course and Exam Description sets how much each section counts toward the composite. The composite is then mapped to a 1 to 5 score using that year's cutoffs.
