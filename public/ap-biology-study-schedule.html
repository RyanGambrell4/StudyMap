AP Biology Study Schedule

The 8-week plan for AP Bio, built on the Fall 2025 Course and Exam Description. Unit 7 (Natural Selection, the highest-weighted unit) gets a full week, Units 3 and 6 get a week each, and the last two weeks go to data analysis, all six free-response question types and two full-length practice exams.

Exam format (from the Fall 2025 CED): 3 hours in total. Section I is 60 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, worth 50% of the score. Section II is 6 free-response questions in 90 minutes, worth 50%: two 9-point questions (Interpreting and Evaluating Experimental Results, and the same with Graphing) and four 4-point questions (Scientific Investigation, Conceptual Analysis, Analyze Model or Visual Representation, and Analyze Data). A four-function, scientific or graphing calculator is allowed on both sections, and you get the AP Biology Equations and Formulas sheet. The 2027 exam is on Monday, May 3, 2027, in the afternoon session.

The 8-week schedule

WeekFocusWhat to actually do
Week 1
Mar 8		Units 1 & 2: Chemistry of Life + CellsWater and hydrogen bonding (cohesion, adhesion, surface tension), elements of life, carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids and proteins. Then cell structure, cell size and surface area-to-volume ratio, the plasma membrane, passive and active transport, tonicity and water potential, compartmentalization and endosymbiosis. 20 multiple-choice questions. Practice identifying molecule types from structural diagrams.
Week 2
Mar 15		Unit 3: Cellular EnergeticsEnzymes and activation energy, environmental effects on enzyme function (temperature, pH, inhibitors, denaturation), cellular energy, photosynthesis (light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle) and cellular respiration (glycolysis, the Krebs cycle, the electron transport chain, fermentation). 12-16% of the multiple-choice section. 20 problems and 1 free-response question.
Week 3
Mar 22		Units 4 & 5: Cell Communication and Cell Cycle + HeredityCell communication, signal transduction pathways, positive and negative feedback, the cell cycle and its regulation (checkpoints, cancer, apoptosis). Then meiosis and genetic diversity, Mendelian and non-Mendelian genetics, pedigrees, and environmental effects on phenotype. Run a chi-square test on a genetic cross with the formula sheet in front of you. 2 timed free-response questions.
Week 4
Mar 29		Unit 6: Gene Expression and RegulationDNA and RNA structure, replication, transcription and RNA processing, translation, regulation of gene expression (including operons), gene expression and cell specialization, mutations, and biotechnology (gel electrophoresis, PCR). 12-16% of the multiple-choice section. 20 problems and 1 free-response question.
Week 5
Apr 5		Unit 7: Natural SelectionNatural and artificial selection, population genetics, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium (use the equations on the formula sheet), evidence of evolution, common ancestry, phylogenetic trees and cladograms, speciation, and the origins of life. At 13-20%, this is the highest-weighted unit, so it gets the full week. 20 problems and 2 free-response questions.
Week 6
Apr 12		Unit 8: EcologyResponses to the environment, energy flow through ecosystems and trophic levels, population ecology (exponential and logistic growth), density effects and carrying capacity, community ecology, biodiversity (Simpson's Diversity Index is on the formula sheet), and disruptions to ecosystems. Take Practice Exam 1 at the end of the week under real timing.
Week 7
Apr 19		Data, statistics and the six free-response typesGraph data with correct axes and error bars, calculate mean, standard deviation and standard error, state null hypotheses and run chi-square tests. Write one of each free-response question type across the week, starting with the two 9-point questions. Redo every question you missed on Practice Exam 1. Take Practice Exam 2 at the end of the week.
Week 8
Apr 26		Review & restShort spaced review across all 8 units, weighted toward the weakest units on your two practice exams. 2 free-response questions per day Mon-Thu with the Equations and Formulas sheet open. Fri-Sun: flashcards and sleep. The exam is Monday afternoon, May 3.

Unit weights on the AP Biology exam

The Fall 2025 CED publishes these weights for the multiple-choice section. Unit 7 has the highest range, followed by Units 3 and 6.

  • Unit 1: Chemistry of Life: 8-11%
  • Unit 2: Cells: 10-13%
  • Unit 3: Cellular Energetics: 12-16%
  • Unit 4: Cell Communication and Cell Cycle: 10-15%
  • Unit 5: Heredity: 8-11%
  • Unit 6: Gene Expression and Regulation: 12-16%
  • Unit 7: Natural Selection: 13-20% (highest weight)
  • Unit 8: Ecology: 10-15%

The free-response section is organized by question type rather than by unit. For quick review of the definitions in each unit, use the AP Biology flashcards, which follow this same framework.

Source: College Board, AP Biology Course and Exam Description, Effective Fall 2025 (unit titles, topics, multiple-choice unit weights, exam format, free-response question types and the Equations and Formulas sheet). 2027 exam date from the College Board's 2027 AP Exam Schedule.

What most AP Bio students get wrong

They over-study memorizable content (photosynthesis diagrams) and under-study experimental design. Four of the six free-response questions are about experimental results, scientific investigation or data analysis, and the CED's science practices include stating null hypotheses, describing data and running statistical tests. Reserve real time for graph reading and experimental analysis.

They skip the AP Biology Equations and Formulas sheet. You get one on exam day. It has the statistics formulas (standard deviation, standard error, chi-square and its table), Hardy-Weinberg, population growth, Simpson's Diversity Index and water potential. Practice with it in front of you every session from week 3 on.

They wait too long to write the two 9-point free-response questions. Those two are worth 18 of the section's points. Practice at least one a week from week 2.

The 4-week compressed version

  • Week 1: Units 1, 2 and 3 (through cellular energetics). 15 multiple-choice questions per unit.
  • Week 2: Units 4, 5 and 6. Practice Exam 1 at the end of the week.
  • Week 3: Units 7 and 8, with three sessions on Unit 7. Data analysis and statistics practice. Practice Exam 2.
  • Week 4: Weak-area targeting Mon-Wed. Rest Thu-Sun.
