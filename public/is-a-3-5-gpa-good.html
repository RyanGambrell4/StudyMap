Is a 3.5 GPA good?

Yes. A 3.5 GPA sits halfway between a B+ (3.3) and an A- (3.7) on the standard 4.0 scale. It clears any graduate program minimum of 3.0 and any employer cutoff set at 3.5. Here is the full picture.

What a 3.5 GPA means

On the University of Chicago grading scale, a B+ is 3.3 and an A- is 3.7, so a 3.5 sits exactly between them. UT Austin counts those grades as 3.33 and 3.67. Grading differs between schools and majors, so the most useful comparison is local: check whether your registrar or department publishes grade distributions.

Whether a 3.5 is "good" depends on what you are trying to do with it. It meets any stated minimum of 3.5 or lower. At programs whose admitted students report a median above 3.5, it is below the middle of the class and needs compensating factors.

Is a 3.5 GPA good for graduate school?

A 3.5 clears any 3.0 minimum. If a graduate program sets a minimum GPA, it says so on its admissions page. For professional and doctoral programs, the picture is more nuanced:

  • MBA programs: Each program publishes a class profile with the GPA of its entering class. Compare your GPA with those figures; GMAT or GRE scores, work experience and essays carry weight alongside it.
  • Law school: T14 median GPAs run from 3.88 to 3.99, and a 3.5 is below the 25th percentile at all 14 (the lowest is 3.74 at Michigan Law). Across all 196 ABA schools, 3.50 is the lower quartile of school medians: about a quarter of schools report a median at or below it, so a 3.5 with a strong LSAT is competitive at many of them.
  • Medical school: Medical schools report the GPAs of their entering classes. Compare your GPA with those figures at each MD and DO program you are considering; the MCAT and clinical experience carry weight alongside it.
  • Master's programs (general): A 3.5 clears the stated minimum at any program that lists 3.0 or 3.5.
  • PhD programs: Departments set their own expectations, and research experience matters alongside GPA. Check the department's admissions page.

Law school figures: ABA Standard 509 disclosures, 2025 entering class (abarequireddisclosures.org).

Is a 3.5 GPA good for jobs?

A 3.5 meets any employer GPA cutoff set at 3.5 or lower. If an employer screens on GPA, the cutoff is usually stated in the posting. If a firm states a higher cutoff, such as 3.7, a 3.5 falls short of it there.

Where GPA is listed but not screened on, a 3.5 is a positive data point that will not work against you.

Is a 3.5 GPA good by major?

Grading differs by field and by school. If your department publishes grade distributions, use them to see where a 3.5 sits in your major, and mention it if the comparison helps you.

How to maintain or build on a 3.5

A 3.5 gives you options. To maintain it, focus your study time on the courses where you are most at risk of a grade drop. Those carry the most GPA leverage. Use the grade calculator to see exactly what you need on remaining assignments to land the grade you want in each course.

To improve from a 3.5, calculate which courses give you the most GPA movement per credit hour. A higher-credit course where you are currently at a B will move your GPA more than a one-credit elective where you are already at an A. Then apply active recall and spaced repetition in those high-leverage courses. In one well-known experiment (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006, Experiment 2), students read a prose passage. A week later, students who studied it once and took three recall tests remembered 61% of its ideas, against 40% for students who studied it four times. On a test 5 minutes after studying, restudying looked better, which is why re-reading feels like it works (Psychological Science, 2006).

Frequently asked questions

Is a 3.5 GPA good?

Yes. A 3.5 sits between a B+ (3.3) and an A- (3.7) on the standard 4.0 scale. It meets any graduate program minimum of 3.0 and any employer cutoff set at 3.5. Selective programs that report higher medians will want compensating strengths.

Is a 3.5 GPA good enough for graduate school?

A 3.5 is above any 3.0 minimum. For selective PhD, MBA and MD programs, compare it with the GPAs each program reports for admitted students. At T14 law schools, where medians run from 3.88 to 3.99, a 3.5 is below the 25th percentile at every school.

Is a 3.5 GPA good for a job?

Yes. A 3.5 meets any employer GPA cutoff set at 3.5 or lower. If an employer screens on GPA, the cutoff is usually stated in the posting. If a firm states a higher cutoff, a 3.5 falls short of it there.

What percentile is a 3.5 GPA?

There is no single percentile for a 3.5, because grading differs between schools and majors. For a real answer, check whether your registrar or department publishes grade distributions, or whether your school reports class rank.

Is a 3.5 GPA good in STEM?

Grading differs by field and by school, so the comparison that counts is within your own department. If it publishes grade distributions, use them to see where a 3.5 sits.

How do I improve from a 3.5 GPA?

Focus on higher-credit courses where you are currently below your target. Those courses move your GPA more than low-credit electives. Use active recall and spaced repetition rather than re-reading.
