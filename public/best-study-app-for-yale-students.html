How do Yale students manage heavy reading loads?

Yale courses often assign 100–300 pages of reading per week. Effective students break readings into active-recall sessions: read a section, close the book, and reconstruct the key argument from memory. StudyEdge AI automates this workflow by generating targeted questions from any uploaded reading.

What study techniques work best at Yale?
