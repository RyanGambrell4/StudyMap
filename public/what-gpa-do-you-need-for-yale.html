What GPA Do You Need for Yale University?

What Yale's own Common Data Set says, and does not say, about GPA for its fall 2025 first-year class.

Yale University does not publish an average high school GPA. Its 2025-26 Common Data Set leaves the GPA section (C12) blank, so any average you see quoted for Yale comes from somewhere other than the school. What it does publish: Yale admitted 4.7% of applicants for fall 2025 (2,387 of 50,264 applicants).

How to read these numbers

Without a published average, the honest comparison is with the admit rate and the factors the school lists. Yale does not publish a minimum GPA in its Common Data Set, and the same document's section C7 lists how much weight the school says it gives to GPA, course rigor and other factors. High schools also calculate GPA differently, so check whether yours is weighted before comparing. The weighted vs unweighted GPA calculator converts between the two.

Questions

What GPA do you need for Yale University?

Yale does not publish an average or minimum high school GPA; the GPA section of its 2025-26 Common Data Set is blank. It admitted 4.7% of applicants for fall 2025.

What is Yale University's acceptance rate?

Yale admitted 4.7% of first-year applicants for fall 2025: 2,387 of 50,264, from its 2025-26 Common Data Set.

Source

Yale University Common Data Set 2025-26, section C (first-time, first-year admission), fall 2025 entering class. Retrieved October 31, 2026. Applicant counts are men plus women; Yale publishes no total row.
