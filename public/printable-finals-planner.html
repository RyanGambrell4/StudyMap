How to fill it in

Start with the overview table. One row per final: course, date and time, room, and how much the final counts. For the Score needed column, run each course through the final exam calculator with your current grade, the weight of the final and the grade you want. Then circle how ready you feel.

Mark your exams on the 7-day grid before anything else. Then give the most blocks to the final with the highest score needed and the lowest readiness. Make the last block before each exam a timed practice test for that course, and don't schedule a course after its exam is done. If you'd rather have the split worked out, enter your finals in the finals week study schedule planner and copy its plan onto the grid.

Use the checklists as you go. List the topics each exam covers, tick off the practice test and formula sheet when they are done, and pack your materials the night before. The day-before list is the same for every final, so run through it once per exam.

If three of your finals fall within two days, read how to study for 3 finals in 2 days for a block plan that fits that squeeze.

Questions

How do I print the finals planner?

Click Print this planner, or use your browser's print command. Choose US Letter, portrait, and default margins. The site menu, buttons and links are left out of the printout, so you get two pages: the overview and 7-day grid, then the exam checklists and the day-before list. To keep a digital copy, choose Save as PDF as the printer.

Where do I get the score needed for each final?

Use the final exam calculator. Enter your current grade, what the final is worth and the grade you want, and it gives the score you need on the final. Write that number in the Score needed column.

How should I fill in the 7-day grid?

Write the date at the top of each column and mark your exams first. Then give the most blocks to the final with the highest score needed and the lowest readiness, and make the last block before each exam a timed practice test for that course. The finals week study schedule planner can work out the split for you.
