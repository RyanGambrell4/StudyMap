What a 3.7 GPA means

A 3.7 is an A- average: consistent high performance across your coursework. Grading differs between schools and majors, so the most useful comparison is local: check whether your registrar or department publishes grade distributions.

Is a 3.7 GPA good for graduate school?

A 3.7 clears any stated minimum of 3.0 or 3.5. If a graduate program sets a minimum GPA, it says so on its admissions page. How it positions you at the most selective programs:

  • Law school: T14 law school medians range from 3.88 (Michigan Law) to 3.99 (University of Virginia School of Law); Yale, Harvard and Stanford report 3.96, 3.96 and 3.96. A 3.7 is below the 25th percentile at every T14 school (the lowest is 3.74 at Michigan Law), so a T14 application at 3.7 depends on a top LSAT; T14 LSAT medians run 170 to 174. Across all 196 ABA schools, the median of school medians is 3.67, so a 3.7 is above the median at about half of them.
  • MBA programs: Each program publishes a class profile with the GPA of its entering class. Compare your GPA with those figures; GMAT or GRE scores, work experience and essays carry weight alongside it.
  • Medical school: Medical schools report the GPAs of their entering classes. Compare your GPA with those figures at each MD and DO program you are considering; the MCAT and clinical experience carry weight alongside it.
  • PhD programs: A 3.7 is a strong GPA for a PhD application, and research experience and recommendations weigh heavily too.
  • Master's programs: A 3.7 clears any minimum of 3.0 or 3.5. Compare it with the median each program reports to see where you stand.

Law school figures: ABA Standard 509 disclosures, 2025 entering class (abarequireddisclosures.org).

Is a 3.7 GPA good for jobs?

A 3.7 clears any employer GPA cutoff set at 3.5 or lower, and it is strong enough to list on a resume. If an employer screens on GPA, the cutoff is usually stated in the posting.

Does a 3.7 GPA qualify for Latin honors?

Each school sets its own Latin honors rules (some by GPA, some by class rank); check your registrar's page. Whether a 3.7 qualifies depends entirely on those rules, so read your school's policy rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

How to maintain a 3.7 or push to 3.8+

A 3.7 is strong, and the strategy for maintaining or improving it is different from recovering from a lower GPA. At this level, the remaining improvements usually come from exam performance rather than missing assignments. The highest-leverage move is converting passive review (re-reading notes) to active recall: testing yourself on material rather than re-exposing yourself to it.

In one well-known experiment (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006, Experiment 2), students read a prose passage. A week later, students who studied it once and took three recall tests remembered 61% of its ideas, against 40% for students who studied it four times. On a test 5 minutes after studying, restudying looked better, which is why re-reading feels like it works (Psychological Science, 2006). At a 3.7 GPA, you are almost certainly doing many things right. The marginal gains come from the quality of exam prep, not the quantity of study hours.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 3.7 GPA good?

Yes. On the common 4.0 scale a 3.7 is an A- average (some schools count an A- as 3.67). It is above the median at about half of ABA law schools. It also clears any employer cutoff set at 3.5.

What letter grade is a 3.7 GPA?

On the common 4.0 scale, a 3.7 is an A-. At the University of Chicago and IU Indianapolis an A- is exactly 3.7. At UT Austin, Penn State and the University of Iowa an A- is 3.67, so a 3.7 sits just above an A- there. The table above lists every grade.

Is a 3.7 GPA good for law school?

For most law schools, yes. Across all 196 ABA-accredited schools, the median of school medians is 3.67, so a 3.7 is above the median at about half of them. It is below the median at every T14 school, where medians run from 3.88 to 3.99, so a T14 application at 3.7 needs a high LSAT.

Is a 3.7 GPA good for medical school?

Medical schools report the GPAs of their entering classes. Compare a 3.7 with those figures at each MD and DO program you are considering; the MCAT carries heavy weight alongside it.

What percentile is a 3.7 GPA?

There is no single percentile for a 3.7, because grading differs between schools and majors. Check whether your registrar or department publishes grade distributions, or whether your school reports class rank.

What honors does a 3.7 GPA earn?

Each school sets its own Latin honors rules (some by GPA, some by class rank); check your registrar's page.

How does a 3.7 compare to a 3.5 GPA?

A 3.7 is two tenths higher: an A- average rather than the midpoint between a B+ and an A-. The gap matters most where a program reports a median that falls between the two numbers. See Is a 3.5 GPA good? for the comparison.

How do I go from a 3.7 to a 4.0?

On a scale where the top grade is an A worth 4.0, a cumulative 4.0 requires an A in every course, so once your GPA is 3.7 you can move closer to 4.0 but not reach it. With 60 credits at 3.7, 60 more credits of straight A's brings you to 3.85. (At a school that awards an A+ above 4.0, such as Iowa's 4.33, the math differs.) Use active recall rather than re-reading for exam prep, practice with past exams under timed conditions, and track your running grade in each class.
