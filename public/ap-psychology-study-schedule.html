AP Psychology Study Schedule

The 8-week plan for AP Psychology, built on the five-unit course framework that replaced the old nine-unit course in 2024-25. Each unit gets one week, because the College Board weights all five the same, and the last three weeks go to research methods, both free-response question types and two practice exams.

The course changed in 2024-25. The AP Psychology Course and Exam Description now has five units, each weighted 15-25% of the multiple-choice section. Research methods are no longer a separate unit: they are a skill (Research Methods and Design) tested across every unit. If your review book lists nine units, starting with "Scientific Foundations of Psychology," it is using the old framework.

Exam format (from the CED): 2 hours 40 minutes in total. Section I is 75 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, worth 66.7% of the score. Section II is 2 free-response questions in 70 minutes, worth 33.3%: the Article Analysis Question (one summarized peer-reviewed study, 25 minutes including a 10-minute reading period) and the Evidence-Based Question (three summarized studies on one topic, 45 minutes including a 15-minute reading period). The 2027 exam is on Friday, May 14, 2027, in the afternoon session.

The 8-week schedule

WeekFocusWhat to actually do
Week 1
Mar 22		Unit 1: Biological Bases of BehaviorInteraction of heredity and environment (twin, family and adoption studies), the nervous system, neurons and neural firing (agonists and antagonists), brain structures and their functions, sleep and its stages, and sensation. Start a habit you will keep all eight weeks: for every study you read, name the research method, the variables and what the results do and do not allow you to conclude. 20 multiple-choice questions.
Week 2
Mar 29		Unit 2: CognitionPerception (bottom-up and top-down processing), thinking, problem-solving, judgments and decision-making, memory from encoding to storage to retrieval, forgetting and other memory challenges, and intelligence and achievement. 20 multiple-choice questions. Write 1 timed Article Analysis Question (25 minutes) on a memory study.
Week 3
Apr 5		Unit 3: Development and LearningThemes and methods in developmental psychology, physical development, gender and sexual orientation, cognitive development (Piaget's schemas, assimilation and accommodation), communication and language, social-emotional development across the lifespan, then classical conditioning, operant conditioning, and social, cognitive and neurological factors in learning. For each learning concept, write your own example in a new scenario, not the textbook one. 20 multiple-choice questions.
Week 4
Apr 12		Unit 4: Social Psychology and PersonalityAttribution theory and person perception, attitude formation and change, the psychology of social situations, psychodynamic and humanistic theories of personality, social-cognitive and trait theories, motivation and emotion. 20 multiple-choice questions. Write 1 timed Evidence-Based Question (45 minutes) on a social psychology topic.
Week 5
Apr 19		Unit 5: Mental and Physical HealthHealth psychology (stress, the general adaptation syndrome, coping), positive psychology, explaining and classifying psychological disorders (the biopsychosocial model), the categories of disorders the CED lists, and treatment approaches. Build a comparison table of the disorder categories and of the treatment approaches. Take Practice Exam 1 at the end of the week (75 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, then both free-response questions in 70 minutes).
Week 6
Apr 26		Research methods and data, across all five unitsExperimental, correlational and other designs, operational definitions, sampling and random assignment, ethical guidelines, and the basic statistics you may be asked to interpret (measures of central tendency, variation and percentile rank, and correlation coefficients). Redo every question you missed on Practice Exam 1, sorted by unit. Write 2 timed Article Analysis Questions: identify the method, variables and ethics, interpret the statistics, say whether the results generalize, and explain how the study supports or refutes the concept.
Week 7
May 3		Evidence-Based Questions, then Practice Exam 2Write 2 timed Evidence-Based Questions: propose a claim, support it with evidence from the sources and from what you learned in the course, and explain your reasoning. Take Practice Exam 2 at the end of the week under full timing, and compare it with Practice Exam 1 by unit.
Week 8
May 10		Review and restMon-Wed: 25 mixed multiple-choice questions a day from your two weakest units, and one free-response question a day, alternating the two types. Thursday: key terms only, then sleep. The exam is Friday afternoon, May 14.

Unit weights on the AP Psychology exam

The CED gives every unit the same weight on the multiple-choice section, so no unit is safe to skip.

  • Unit 1: Biological Bases of Behavior: 15-25%
  • Unit 2: Cognition: 15-25%
  • Unit 3: Development and Learning: 15-25%
  • Unit 4: Social Psychology and Personality: 15-25%
  • Unit 5: Mental and Physical Health: 15-25%

The multiple-choice section is also weighted by skill: about 65% Concept Application, 25% Research Methods and Design and 10% Data Interpretation. The two free-response questions are worth 16.65% of the score each. For quick review of the definitions in each unit, use the AP Psychology flashcards, which follow this same framework.

Source: College Board, AP Psychology Course and Exam Description, Effective Fall 2025 (the framework introduced in 2024-25: unit titles, topics, multiple-choice unit and skill weights, exam format and free-response question types). 2027 exam date from the College Board's 2027 AP Exam Schedule.

Where AP Psychology students lose points

They study from the old course. Review books written before 2024-25 have nine units, a 100-question multiple-choice section and different free-response questions. Much of the psychology overlaps, but the unit list, the weights and the free-response tasks have all changed. Check that your practice exams have 75 multiple-choice questions and the two current free-response types.

They memorize names instead of applying concepts. Concept Application is about 65% of the multiple-choice section, and the CED's sample questions describe a new situation and ask which concept it shows, like the sample about a perfume that becomes a conditioned stimulus. For every term you learn, write one example of your own.

They treat research methods as a one-week topic. Research Methods and Design is about a quarter of the multiple-choice section, and the Article Analysis Question asks you to identify the method, variables and ethical guidelines in a real study and interpret its statistics. Practice spotting why a conclusion does or does not follow, for example a causal claim drawn from a correlational design.

They summarize the sources in the Evidence-Based Question instead of arguing. The task is to propose a claim and support it with evidence from the sources and reasoning about that evidence. Decide your claim during the reading period, then use the sources to back it up.

They confuse similar concepts. Classical and operant conditioning, negative reinforcement and punishment, reliability and validity, and encoding and retrieval are easy to swap under time pressure. Make a side-by-side table for each pair and test yourself on scenarios.

The 4-week compressed version

  • Week 1: Units 1 and 2. 15 multiple-choice questions per unit. One Article Analysis Question.
  • Week 2: Units 3 and 4. Apply every learning and social psychology concept to a new scenario. One Evidence-Based Question. Practice Exam 1 at the end of the week.
  • Week 3: Unit 5, then research methods and statistics review. One of each free-response type, timed. Practice Exam 2 at the end of the week.
  • Week 4: Weak-unit review Mon-Wed using both practice exam error logs. Light review Thursday, then the exam on Friday, May 14.
