Is a 3 on AP Macroeconomics Good?

What a 3, 4 or 5 on the AP Macroeconomics exam means, how many students earned each score in 2026, and when the 2027 exam is.

Yes, a 3 on AP Macroeconomics is a passing score. The College Board calls a 3 "qualified". In 2026, 25% of AP Macroeconomics students scored exactly 3, and 66% scored 3 or higher. A 3 meets the score the College Board recommends colleges accept for credit, but each college decides for itself.

What a 4 or a 5 on AP Macroeconomics means

The College Board describes a 4 as "very well qualified" and a 5 as "extremely well qualified". In 2026, 22% of AP Macroeconomics students earned a 4 and 19% earned a 5. A 2 ("possibly qualified") went to 20% of students and a 1 ("no recommendation") to 14%.

Will a 3 on AP Macroeconomics get you college credit?

The College Board and the American Council on Education recommend that colleges and universities award credit for AP scores of 3 or higher. That is a recommendation, not a rule. Colleges set their own policies, and the score a college wants can differ by subject. Look up the exact policy for each college on your list with the College Board's AP credit policy search.

When is the 2027 AP Macroeconomics exam?

The College Board has scheduled the 2027 AP Macroeconomics exam for Friday, May 7, 2027, in the afternoon session. The College Board says the two daily sessions are typically morning and afternoon, and your AP coordinator confirms the exact time and room.
