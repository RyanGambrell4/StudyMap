What GPA Do You Need for Georgia Tech?

The average high school GPA and admit rate Georgia Tech reported for its fall 2025 first-year class, straight from its Common Data Set.

First-year students who enrolled at Georgia Tech in fall 2025 had an average high school GPA of 4.17, according to the school's 2025-26 Common Data Set (95.31% of them reported a GPA). The Common Data Set does not say whether this is weighted or unweighted, but an average above 4.0 is only possible if weighted GPAs are included. Georgia Tech admitted 13.3% of applicants (8,921 of 66,881 applicants).

How to read these numbers

These are the GPAs of students who enrolled, not a cutoff. Georgia Tech does not publish a minimum GPA in its Common Data Set, and the same document's section C7 lists how much weight the school says it gives to GPA, course rigor and other factors. High schools also calculate GPA differently, so check whether yours is weighted before comparing. The weighted vs unweighted GPA calculator converts between the two.

Questions

What GPA do you need for Georgia Tech?

There is no published minimum. First-year students who enrolled in fall 2025 averaged a 4.17 high school GPA, according to Georgia Tech's 2025-26 Common Data Set.

What is Georgia Tech's acceptance rate?

Georgia Tech admitted 13.3% of first-year applicants for fall 2025: 8,921 of 66,881, from its 2025-26 Common Data Set.

Is Georgia Tech's average GPA weighted or unweighted?

The Common Data Set does not say. Because the average is above 4.0, it must include weighted GPAs.
