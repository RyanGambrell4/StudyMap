Common questions about switching from Q-Chat

Why did Quizlet shut down Q-Chat?
Q-Chat was retired after June 30, 2025, according to third-party reports. Quizlet has not published a detailed explanation. The feature was expensive to maintain and Quizlet ultimately decided the risks weren't worth it for their platform.
Can I import my Quizlet decks into StudyEdge AI?
There's no direct Quizlet import, but StudyEdge AI generates new, better-organized flashcard sets from your course topics in seconds. The AI creates cards from what you are studying, not generic content from other students' decks.
Is StudyEdge AI free?
There is a free plan with no credit card required: 1 course, 5 AI actions a month, and one try of each premium tool. Pro ($9.99 a month or $69.99 a year) covers up to 5 courses with 100 AI actions a month, and Unlimited ($14.99 a month) removes the limits. A 7-day free trial of Pro is available.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The web app works in any phone browser, and you can add it to your home screen on iOS or Android. A native iOS app is coming soon.
